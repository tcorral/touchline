# MIDI soundtrack

All audio is synthesized locally through Web Audio from bundled standard MIDI files.

- `assets/audio/match_music.mid`: original D minor arena-rock-inspired anthem, played at about 160 BPM with a synthesized kick-and-snare backbeat. Its melody and arrangement are original.
- `assets/audio/kick.mid`: short kick cue.
- `assets/audio/goal.mid`: scoring fanfare.
- `assets/audio/whistle_start.mid`: two-note kickoff whistle.
- `assets/audio/whistle_end.mid`: three-note full-time whistle.

Click **Enable sound** once in the browser, then play. Kickoff whistle plays when the match begins after the countdown. Full-time whistle plays once when the clock expires; the background music stops. Restart resets both whistle cues.
